CHOICE OF THE DAY:FIRST


My Hero Academia is a Japanese anime series set in a world where most people have superpowers known as “Quirks.”The story follows Izuku Midoriya, a Quirkless boy who dreams of becoming a great hero. After inheriting the power of the world’s greatest hero, All Might, he enrols at U.A. High School to train alongside other aspiring heroes. Throughout his journey, Izuku faces powerful villains, builds lasting friendships, and learns what it truly means to be a hero.

CHOICE OF THE DAY:SECOND


Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood is a Japanese anime series set in a world where alchemy is a powerful science used to transform matter. The story follows brothers Edward and Alphonse Elric, who attempt to use alchemy to bring their deceased mother back to life, resulting in Edward losing limbs and Alphonse losing his entire body. Determined to restore themselves, the brothers search for the Philosopher’s Stone. Throughout their journey, they uncover dark government secrets, face dangerous enemies, and discover the true consequences of alchemy.

CHOICE OF THE DAY:THIRD


Attack on Titan is a Japanese anime series set in a world where humanity lives inside enormous walled cities to protect themselves from giant humanoid creatures known as Titans. The story follows Eren Yeager, a young boy who vows to destroy all Titans after witnessing the destruction of his hometown and the death of his mother. Alongside his friends Mikasa Ackerman and Armin Arlert, he joins the military to fight against the Titans. Throughout their journey, they uncover shocking secrets about their world, face devastating battles, and discover the truth behind humanity's existence.
