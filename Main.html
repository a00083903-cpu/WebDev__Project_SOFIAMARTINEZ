My Hero Academia is a Japanese anime series set in a world where most people have superpowers known as “Quirks.”

The story follows Izuku Midoriya, a Quirkless boy who dreams of becoming a great hero. After inheriting the power of the world’s greatest hero, All Might, he enrols at U.A. High School to train alongside other aspiring heroes. Throughout his journey, Izuku faces powerful villains, builds lasting friendships, and learns what it truly means to be a hero.
